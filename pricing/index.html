Common Questions

Pricing, answered.

What does corporate photography cost in Singapore?

Starting from $250 for the Standard tier (up to 2 hours). Most corporate and brand-day shoots land in the Enhanced tier, from $450 for 3–5 hours. Full-day campaign coverage is $850 (Deluxe).

How much does an event photographer cost in Singapore?

Event coverage follows the same three tiers — from $250 for a short event up to 2 hours, $450 for 3–5 hours, or $850 for full-day coverage.

How quickly are edited photographs delivered?

7–14 days on the Standard tier, using an Accurova.AI-optimised workflow. Enhanced and Deluxe both get priority delivery, faster than Standard.

What's included in every package?

Commercial usage rights, optimised colour-accurate post-production, and opt-in photo lab printing — on every tier, no exceptions.
