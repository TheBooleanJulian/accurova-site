Trust & Privacy

How your data and images are handled.

A summary of Accurova's real commitments on data protection, image confidentiality, AI use and copyright. This is a plain-language summary, not a substitute for the full contract terms agreed before each shoot.

Data Protection

PDPA-compliant.

Only the data necessary for project execution and invoicing is collected. It is never sold, traded, or shared with third parties.

Confidentiality

Pre-release material stays private.

For unreleased merchandise, branding, or cosplay debuts, raw files and drafts are not published anywhere without the client's explicit consent.

Responsible AI Use

AI assists the workflow; it doesn't fabricate the work.

Commissioned media, proprietary designs, and personal likenesses are never used to train third-party generative AI without explicit written opt-in. AI tools (generative fill, automated masking) are used only for localized retouching in Photoshop/Lightroom, to enhance human-directed work — not to fabricate the core image.

Contracting

Clear terms before the shutter clicks.

Contracting is aligned with the Tripartite Standard on Contracting with Self-Employed Persons (SEPs) — clear milestones, documented deliverables, timelines and revision terms agreed before shooting starts.

Copyright & Licensing

You get usage rights; Accurova keeps portfolio rights.

Clients receive agreed licensing/usage rights on full payment. Accurova retains the right to display final, publicly-released images in its own portfolio, unless an NDA is in place.

Digital Products

Presets, templates and educational materials.

Accurova also sells digital products — Lightroom presets, CapCut templates, and educational materials — as final sale, licensed for individual use only, with no resale or redistribution.
